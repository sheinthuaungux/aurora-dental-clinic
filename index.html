Premium Solutions

The All-on-4 Treatment Concept

A revolutionary, scientifically validated technique that supports a full arch of new, custom teeth on just four precision-placed titanium implants.

Engineered for Maximum Support

By tilting the posterior implants at a 45-degree angle, we unlock maximum stability and eliminate the need for costly and painful bone grafting. This state-of-the-art procedure allows for immediate load capabilities.

  • Immediate Permanence
  • Same-Day Restoration
  • Preserves Bone & Contour
